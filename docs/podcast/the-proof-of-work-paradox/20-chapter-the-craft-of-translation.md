Why would an engineer spend hundreds of hours writing interactive HTML tutorials, ADRs, and visual architecture diagrams when he could just be writing code?

**SAM:**
Because of the **Mech Suit Methodology**.
In [`E:\work\steppe-strategy\PLAN.md`](file:///E:/work/steppe-strategy/PLAN.md), Derek laid out his life vision:
> *"A multi-decade institutional answer to the question 'I want more people like you' — a system that recognizes and equips the working-class engineers who came up the way you did, via methodology, receipts, training, and books, without gating any of it behind credentials they don't have."*
He wasn't documenting the system to brag about it. He was documenting it so that the next engineer—someone without a computer science degree from Stanford, someone working in a machine shop or an IT support desk—could follow the trail of breadcrumbs and understand how to build their own autonomous lab.
