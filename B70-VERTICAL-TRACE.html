Concurrency truth (corrects the remembered baseline): the June-29 ladder's ~1% was single-card vs layer-split placement, not 4-vs-16 slots. Its actual concurrency finding: at ~14k-token prompts, batch time scales linearly with client count (1→22.9s, 2→45.3s, 4→91s, 8→181.5s) — prefill serializes everything. More slots only help decode-heavy small-prompt traffic; for door-sized payloads the next lever is scheduling/prompt-caching/admission control, not -np. Raising moe slots would also shrink per-slot context (-np divides -c) or cost KV VRAM the cards don't have spare under topology A.
