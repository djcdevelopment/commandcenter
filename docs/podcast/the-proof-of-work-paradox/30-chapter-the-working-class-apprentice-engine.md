**ALEX:**

"A multi-decade institutional answer to the question: 'I want more people like you.'"

**ALEX:**

And when he transitioned into software engineering over twenty years ago, he brought that exact same blue-collar ethos with him: *you make the machine tell the truth, you leave a clear trail, and you don't pretend you did work you didn't do.*

**SAM:**

**ALEX:**

It is an ambient condition of existence: an unbroken loop of *Awareness $\to$ Observation $\to$ Interaction $\to$ Response*. When you ride, you're reading pavement grain, micro-adjusting center of mass, and anticipating traction limits. When you write code, you feel the clock cycles, you respect the silicon, and you eliminate branch stalls before they hit the interpreter. You don't have to start training, because you never step off the field.
