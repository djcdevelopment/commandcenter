### Where this picks up

The previous episode ended with the operator being right and the instruments catching up to him.
Every ceiling turned out to be one somebody had installed. That was the discovery.

This episode is what happened next, and it has a different shape. The findings had to be made
operational — baked into the configuration, the gates, and the register that this lab cites from.
Seven hours went into that and not one new number was produced.

And then the machinery built to keep the work honest started firing on the person who built it.
Six separate times, in six different ways, on the same afternoon. Every time, it was right.

That is the through-line: **a lab is only as honest as the instruments it lets catch itself.**
